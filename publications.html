Publications#

Peer-reviewed publications that describe or use gridops and its predecessor, the GO grid operations model, listed by year. If you have published work that uses gridops, please open an issue so we can add it to this list.

2026#

  • Broman, D., Voisin, N., Steinschneider, S., Kern, J., Ssembatya, H., & Wi, S. (2026). How Hydropower Operations Mitigate Flow Forecast Uncertainties to Maintain Grid Services in the Western U.S. Water Resources Research, 62(2), e2025WR040943. https://doi.org/10.1029/2025WR040943

  • Mongird, K., Akdemir, K. Z., Bracken, C., Burleyson, C. D., Thurber, T., Vernon, C. R., Zhao, M., & Rice, J. S. (2026). Energy Infrastructure Futures: A Multiscale Evaluation of Projected Power Plant Siting Across the Western Interconnection. Earth’s Future, 14(8), e2025EF007725. https://doi.org/10.1029/2025EF007725

  • Ssembatya, H., Kern, J. D., Voisin, N., Steinschneider, S., & Broman, D. (2026). The relative influences of hydrologic information and dams’ hydropower scheduling decisions on electricity price forecasts. Energy Reports, 15, 109203. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.egyr.2026.109203

2025#

  • Akdemir, K. Z., Mongird, K., Bracken, C., Burleyson, C. D., Kern, J. D., Oikonomou, K., Thurber, T. B., Vernon, C. R., Voisin, N., Zhao, M., & Rice, J. S. (2025). Advances in Applied Energy, 20, 100249. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adapen.2025.100249

  • Akdemir, K. Z., Mongird, K., Kern, J. D., Oikonomou, K., Voisin, N., Burleyson, C. D., Rice, J. S., Zhao, M., Bracken, C., & Vernon, C. (2025). Investigating the effects of cooperative transmission expansion planning on grid performance during heat waves with varying spatial scales. Applied Energy, 378, 124825. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.124825

  • Zeighami, A., Kern, J. D., Bracken, C., Burleyson, C. D., & Peng, W. (2025). The effects of renewable energy scaling on sub-annual air pollution emission dynamics from the power sector: A Western U.S. case study. Environmental Research: Energy, 2(4), 045004. https://doi.org/10.1088/2753-3751/ae123b

2024#

  • Akdemir, K. Z., Oikonomou, K., Kern, J. D., Voisin, N., Ssembatya, H., & Qian, J. (2024). An open-source framework for balancing computational speed and fidelity in production cost models. Environmental Research: Energy, 1(1), 015003. https://doi.org/10.1088/2753-3751/ad1751
